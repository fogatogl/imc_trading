# Round 5 — Volatility Spike Study

Generated by [`round5/vol_spikes.py`](../../../vol_spikes.py).

## Method

For each product × day, a **spike** at tick `t` is `|ret_1_t| ≥ K · σ_t`, with
`K = 4` and `σ_t = std(ret_1).rolling(500).shift(1)` (long lookback,
shifted by 1 to remove look-ahead and to keep the baseline robust against
persistent high-vol regimes). Detection is per-day so the rolling std doesn't
bridge day boundaries.

For each spike we record sign, magnitude, z-score, and the contemporaneous
spread / depth. Post-spike profile = mean of `−sign · (mid_{t+h} − mid_t)`
for `h ∈ {1, 5, 10, 50, 200}`. **Positive ⇒ reversion** (price moves *against*
the spike). **Negative ⇒ momentum follow-through**.

Cross-product co-occurrence: for each spike at `(product, t)`, count how many
*other* products spiked within `±2` ticks. Reported per product as
`mean_peer_count` and `systemic_rate = P(peer_count ≥ 5)`.

## Headline findings

### 1. Spikes are concentrated in 4 products

| Product | n_spikes | rate / 10k ticks | z_mean | z_max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ROBOT_DISHES | 117 | 39.0 | 4.66 | 9.4 |
| OXYGEN_SHAKE_CHOCOLATE | 60 | 20.0 | 6.12 | 9.7 |
| OXYGEN_SHAKE_EVENING_BREATH | 56 | 18.7 | 5.26 | 10.8 |
| ROBOT_IRONING | 50 | 16.7 | 5.66 | 8.7 |
| _everything else (46 products)_ | _125 total_ | _~1 / 10k_ | — | — |

These 4 products account for **~70% of all 4σ events on the round-5 universe**.
Four other products (`MICROCHIP_RECTANGLE`, `MICROCHIP_TRIANGLE`,
`MICROCHIP_SQUARE`, `MICROCHIP_OVAL`) form a second tier (10–18 spikes each).
The remaining 42 products have ≤4 spikes across all 3 days × 30k ticks —
spikes are essentially absent.

### 2. Spikes are **idiosyncratic**, not systemic

Mean peer-count across all spike events ≈ **0.15** — when one product spikes,
on average 0.15 *other* products spike within ±2 ticks. `systemic_rate = 0.0`
for every product with `n_spikes ≥ 10`. There is no cross-family contagion
event in the data; the round-5 universe does not move in lockstep, even in
the tails.

**Implication**: there is no spike-driven hedge / portfolio trade. Each
product's spike behaviour stands on its own.

### 3. The big-4 spikers mean-revert strongly post-spike

Per-spike signed cumret (positive = revert):

| Product | h=1 | h=5 | h=10 | h=50 | h=200 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ROBOT_DISHES (n=117) | +28.7 | +36.3 | **+43.2** | +42.6 | +41.7 |
| OXYGEN_SHAKE_CHOCOLATE (n=60) | +28.7 | +40.4 | **+38.2** | +42.9 | +31.9 |
| OXYGEN_SHAKE_EVENING_BREATH (n=56) | +23.5 | +33.9 | **+37.0** | +37.7 | +46.2 |
| ROBOT_IRONING (n=50) | +23.6 | +33.2 | **+36.0** | +45.4 | +57.3 |

A `+40` reversion at h=10 versus a typical spread of ~2 ticks is a
~20× edge per spike. These four are already classified `MR_TAKER` with
`mr_confidence=high` and FDR-significant `neg_zscore_mid_50` IC in the
existing pipeline ([archetype assignment](../../ROBOT/archetype_assignment.csv),
[OXYGEN_SHAKE archetype](../../OXYGEN_SHAKE/archetype_assignment.csv)) —
the spike study **quantifies the magnitude** of the tail reversion that
the rolling-z-score taker captures.

### 4. Spreads barely widen during spikes — MM cushion holds

Family-level `spread_widen_x = at_spike_spread / baseline_spread`:

| Family | widen ×  | depth × |
|---|---:|---:|
| ROBOT | 1.08 | 0.95 |
| OXYGEN_SHAKE | 1.07 | 0.97 |
| MICROCHIP | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| PEBBLES | 1.07 | 1.03 |
| _all families_ | 0.84–1.12 | 0.84–1.06 |

Spread widens only ~6–10 % during spikes on the spike-heavy families and
depth at the L1 quote actually *increases* on PEBBLES / MICROCHIP_TRIANGLE.
**Passive MM is not driven away by the spike**; the dislocation is a
crossing-trade event, not a book evaporation. A spike-fade taker on the
big-4 products is taking against a still-well-quoted book.

### 5. Caveat: "spike" on the big-4 ≈ "ordinary tick" elsewhere

ROBOT_DISHES has `acf_lag1 = −0.232` and `vr_k5 = 0.555` — its mid oscillates
every tick. The spike threshold (`|ret_1| ≥ 4σ_500`) catches the same
oscillation that `neg_zscore_mid_50` is already trading. The post-spike
reversion isn't a tail-only phenomenon; it's the body of the same MR
distribution. This is good news for the existing MR_TAKER strategy
(no separate "extreme regime" needed), and bad news for any
hypothesis that vol clusters create *new* alpha on top of the always-on
mean-reversion these four products already exhibit.

### 6. MICROCHIP_SQUARE — only post-spike *momentum* in the universe

MICROCHIP_SQUARE (n=10) is the one product with material spike count and
a *negative* h=10 post-spike mean (−33 ticks). Sample is too small for a
clean conclusion (n=10 across 3 days), but the existing archetype shows it
as `MR_TAKER` with `vr_k5 = 0.960` (only mildly mean-reverting) and *no*
FDR-significant IC. It may behave more like a trending product after a
shock — worth a targeted dive (
[`figures/MICROCHIP_SQUARE_spike_panel.png`](figures/MICROCHIP_SQUARE_spike_panel.png))
before sizing it the same way as other MR_TAKERs.

### 7. Other families' n is too small to interpret

GALAXY_SOUNDS (8), SLEEP_POD (10), PEBBLES (7), TRANSLATOR (9), PANEL (9),
SNACKPACK (8), UV_VISOR (17) — sample sizes per family are 7–17 events
across 3 days. Family-level "post-spike momentum" rows in
[`family_summary.csv`](family_summary.csv) for these are dominated by
1–2 large outliers each and should not be treated as signal.

## Trading-usage summary

1. **Already-priced edge** — ROBOT_DISHES / IRONING and
   OXYGEN_SHAKE_CHOCOLATE / EVENING_BREATH are the four products where
   strong tail reversion exists. The current `MR_TAKER` template
   (`neg_zscore_mid_50` taker, vol-armored sizing) already captures it.
   The spike study confirms the magnitude (~40 ticks per event at h=10)
   and that the book stays liquid during the spike, so the taker is not
   crossing into a hollowed-out queue.
2. **No portfolio / cross-product hedge** — spikes do not co-occur across
   products (peer-count ≈ 0.15, systemic_rate = 0). A "fade the universe
   when N products spike" trade has no support in the data.
3. **MM regime gate is unnecessary on the big-4** — spreads do not widen
   meaningfully during the spike; the existing MR taker need not stand
   down or widen quotes. (This is a *negative* result for the
   `MM_RISK_HIGH_VOL` flag on these products.)
4. **MICROCHIP_SQUARE deserves a separate look** — only spike-cluster product
   in the universe whose post-spike behaviour leans momentum rather than
   mean-reversion. n=10 is small but the existing classifier flagged it
   with weak FDR support; a manual review of
   [`figures/MICROCHIP_SQUARE_spike_panel.png`](figures/MICROCHIP_SQUARE_spike_panel.png)
   is worth doing before submitting it as MR.
5. **42 products have effectively no spike alpha** — vol-conditioned
   trading rules (e.g. spike-fade) do not apply outside the eight
   products in the spike-heavy + second tier. Any vol-regime gate should
   be calibrated *only* to those.
